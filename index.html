About me

Hands-on transformer engineer with production leadership experience.

I'm an electrical engineer specialising in distribution transformers, from design and coil winding through to high-voltage testing, commissioning and repair. Over eight years at Sena Kalyan Electric Industries in Bangladesh, I grew from Assistant Engineer to Deputy Manager (Engineering), leading production teams that delivered record output and executing high-capacity substation projects.

Since July 2026 I've been working as an electrical engineer at Ironcore Transformers in Preston, Victoria, across transformer design, production, testing, QC and repair, while studying a Master of Engineering (Electrical & Electronic) at CQUniversity.

I'm seeking to grow my career in Australia's power, utilities, manufacturing and infrastructure sectors, with a focus on transformers and power systems.

Featured: my engineering app

Personal project · Built 2026

Hashib Transformer Design

A mobile-first engineering app I built to digitise and work with transformer designs. It turns hand-written design sheets into a searchable, costed design library, with tools to check, build and learn every design.

60+designs digitised from hand-written sheets
5modules: Designs, Catalogue, Railway, Calculator, SKEI library
3Dinteractive core & coil model
  • Design search by model number, alternative name, voltage or rating, with full winding data and input/output summary
  • Design calculator that checks flux density, current density, regulation, efficiency and temperature rise, and explains each result
  • Cost estimator with copper, core lamination and labour breakdown, exportable for quoting
  • Wiring diagrams, former & coil build sheets and a 3D core-and-coil view
  • Step-by-step manufacturing procedures with progress checklists and safety notes
  • Read-a-sheet: photograph a hand-written design sheet to capture it into the library
  • Built-in tutor to ask questions about any design, plus photos, backup and offline use
